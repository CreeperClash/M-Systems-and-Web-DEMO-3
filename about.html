Purremium Dining

Our house, their rules

A café that happens to be a cat's living room.

Est. 2019 on Sundridge Lane. Twelve residents, one very small roastery, and a rule that nobody gets picked up unless they ask first.

We started in a narrow shopfront with two rescues, a second-hand espresso machine, and no real plan beyond making somewhere calm.

Everything here is built around the cats first — low light, soft corners, quiet hours before noon, and shelves at exactly the height a cat likes to judge you from. The coffee got good because we had the time. The pastries got good because Nutmeg kept stealing them.

Every resident came from local rescue, and most are looking for a home. Some have been looking for a while. They seem fine about it.

Ninety-two cats have gone home with someone since we opened. We keep a photo of every one of them behind the counter, which makes the wall a slightly emotional place to queue.

  • Let sleeping cats sleep — no waking, no lifting.
  • Hands washed on the way in and on the way out.
  • Under-8s visit with a grown-up, indoor voices please.
  • No flash photography, ever.
