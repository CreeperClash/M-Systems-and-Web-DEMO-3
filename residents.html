Who you'll actually meet

Our furry friends are out for the taking! Enjoy your time with them!

Management

Marmalade

Owns the shelf by the window. Will accept one chin scratch, then look away as if it never happened.

Pastry theft

Nutmeg

Nine years old, entirely food-motivated. Guard your cardamom bun. She is patient and she is watching.

Greeting

Pepper

First to the door, every time. The friendliest cat we've had, and the only one who likes the espresso grinder.

Naps, senior

Biscuit

Fourteen and retired. Sleeps in the armchair nearest the radiator. That armchair is not available. Sorry.

Security

Clove

Two years old and convinced the front door needs supervising. Has never once caught anything.

Lap services

Saffron

Will find the one person in the room who said they're not a cat person and sit on them for an hour.

Quiet hours only

Bramble

Shy, and worth the wait. Comes out before 10am when the room is still. Bonded with Fig, adopted together.

Quiet hours only

Fig

Bramble's brother and slightly braver about it. Likes the top of the bookcase and the sound of the milk steamer.

Supervision

Miso

Watches the pass like a head chef. Has never once approved of a plate leaving the kitchen.

Morale

Pickle

Loud, delighted and entirely without shame. Will tell you about his day whether you asked or not.

Night shift

Juniper

Sleeps through service and patrols the shelves after close. We only have her word for what happens then.

Stair tax

Waffle

Sits precisely halfway up the stairs and expects a toll. The toll is being talked to for a moment.

Adoption

Most of them are looking for a home.
